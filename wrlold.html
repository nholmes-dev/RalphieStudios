The Vision

Not a reskin. Built for Wheelchair Rugby League.

The aim is to make a sports game that feels specific to the real thing: chair momentum, body position, passing under pressure, hard collisions, team shape, player individuality and the controlled chaos that makes Wheelchair Rugby League brilliant to watch and play.

01 / MOVEMENT

Chair-first handling

Acceleration, momentum, steering, pushing and body position designed around the behaviour of a sports wheelchair.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Real match systems

Passing, kicking, possession, tries, tackling, tags, tipping and restarts developed as connected gameplay systems.

03 / PLAYERS

Attributes you can feel

Speed, acceleration, agility, handling, tackling and other ratings are intended to change how individual players actually behave on court.

04 / IDENTITY

Built from the community

Player personalities, chairs, kits, venues and presentation designed to give the sport and its people a genuine identity.

Development diary · 2026
Chair Mechanics Latest Update

Body Position Matters

Leaning is now a gameplay system rather than simply an animation. Players can move their upper body in any direction, with their Agility rating controlling how much lateral movement they can achieve.

Agility affects lean range Full directional right-stick control Body position will affect tag reach
Leaning & Agility · Development Update 001
Chair Handling Working In-Game

Using Your Weight

Body position now affects how the chair behaves. Lean forward while sprinting for a small speed advantage, or lean into a turn to make the chair rotate more aggressively.

Forward-lean sprint boost Lean-assisted turning Analogue controller handling
Chair Handling · Development Update 002
Up Next Stability

Finding the Tipping Point

The next major chair system will combine speed, steering angle, body position and contact to determine when the chair becomes unstable — and whether the player can recover before tipping.

High-speed turning risk Lean affects stability Falls feed into tackling

Development Roadmap

From prototype to match day.

Development is focused on playable systems first. The chair needs to feel right before contact, tactics, full teams and presentation are layered on top.

Phase 01

Core Movement

Acceleration, momentum, sprinting, steering, push animation and fundamental wheelchair control.

Playable
Phase 02

Chair & Body Mechanics

Agility-based leaning, body positioning, advanced turning, stability, tipping and physical chair behaviour.

In development now
Phase 03

Match Systems

Passing, kicking, possession, tries, scoreboard, game clock, tackles, tags and match restarts.

Partially playable
Phase 04

5v5 & Presentation

Teams, AI, switching, full rosters, venues, broadcast presentation, crowds and complete match-day structure.
