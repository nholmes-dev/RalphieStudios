The Vision

Not a reskin. Built for wheelchair rugby league.

The aim is to make a sports game that feels specific to the real game: chair momentum, passing under pressure, hard collisions, team shape, player individuality and the controlled chaos that makes wheelchair rugby league brilliant to watch and play.

01 / MOVEMENT

Chair-first handling

Acceleration, momentum, turning grip and pushing animations designed around the feel of a sports wheelchair — not a standing rugby character.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Ball & contact systems

Possession, passing, kicking, tackles, tries and collisions developed as core playable systems rather than visual dressing.

03 / MODES

Team or player

Control the whole side, switch with the play, or stay locked to one player for a more personal match experience.

04 / IDENTITY

Real personalities

Player attributes, signature traits, individual chairs, kits and presentation designed to make each roster feel different.

This is the feeling

Fast.
Physical.
Unpredictable.

Every push should matter. Every angle should matter. Every hit should change the next decision. The game is being built around momentum and interaction so matches do not feel like the same sequence repeating over and over.

Development

From prototype to match day.

The project is being built in playable layers: make the core movement feel right, then add match systems, teams, presentation and polish without losing the physical feel underneath.
